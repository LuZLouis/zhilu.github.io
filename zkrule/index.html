What the auditor ends up knowing审核机构最终知道了什么
  • One bit — hit or no hit — plus the message length. Not where the word appears, not how often, and none of the other text.只有“命中 / 未命中”这一位，以及消息长度。它不知道违禁词出现在哪里、出现了几次，也看不到其余文字。
  • Every wire value it opens during a spot check is masked with fresh randomness, so on its own it is just a random number. Each round in which every checked gate holds halves a cheater's chance of slipping through.它抽查时打开的每个线值都事先加了随机遮掩，单独看只是随机数；而每一轮被抽查的门都对得上，作弊者漏网的概率就会减半。
  • The full system covers AES-256-GCM encryption, salted commitments, text-format checks and a full-text rule scan in one proof — about 0.05 s per short message.完整的系统在同一个证明里覆盖 AES-256-GCM 加密、带盐承诺、文本格式检查和全文规则扫描，每条短消息约 0.05 秒。
